# Prompt: close out a piece of work

You are doing **closure housekeeping**. Read the project's `AGENTS.md` and
local closure rules. Confirm the required verification, review, and owner
acceptance before closing. Stop if a required step is missing.

Record the final verification, any review verdict, and any owner acceptance
and date. Link the outcome in the plan's Result, move or mark the plan per
the local lifecycle, and update the plan index and current status snapshot
where used. Limit changes to closure.

Follow the local verification contract, including
[closure verification](../docs/VERIFICATION.md#closure-verification) and the
claims audit for substantial work.
Check links affected by any move and review the closure diff. Apply the
delivery contract for closure and report delivery state and the exact final
revision where applicable.
